Arit Mukherjee

Hi! I'm Arit, a high school student in Dubai and a member of the UAE national team in World Schools debating.

I have represented the UAE at international championships from Panama to Kenya. In 2026 our team reached the octofinals of the World Schools Debating Championships and finished 15th in the world, the best result the UAE has ever achieved. Along the way I have picked up speaker awards at tournaments such as Transatlantic WSDC, where I was 2nd Best Overall Speaker, and Doxbridge Pre-WSDC, where I placed 4th of 366 open speakers.

I also adjudicate. I was selected from a pool of 35 judges to sit on the Grand Final panel of the Asia Pacific World Schools Debating Championships, and I have chaired finals at the UAE National Debating Competition.
